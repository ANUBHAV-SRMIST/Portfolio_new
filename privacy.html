Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Overview

This is the personal portfolio website of Anubhav Mishra (anubhav-mishra.vercel.app). I collect as little information as possible. The site has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics trackers.

Information you send me

If you use the contact form, the details you enter (your name, email address, subject and message) are sent to me by email through the EmailJS service. I use them only to reply to you, and I do not sell or share them.

Stored on your device

The site saves your light/dark theme choice in your browser's local storage so it is remembered on your next visit. It stays on your device, and you can clear it any time from your browser settings. The site may also cache its own files for faster loading and offline use.

Third-party services

To work, the site loads resources from or connects to these services, which may receive your IP address and browser details under their own policies:

  • Vercel (website hosting)
  • Google Fonts, Font Awesome (cdnjs), jsDelivr (fonts, icons and scripts)
  • GitHub (public profile statistics shown on the page)
  • EmailJS (delivering contact form messages)

External links (GitHub, LinkedIn, certificates, project demos) lead to sites I do not control, and their privacy practices apply there.
